# Common Terms

## Backtesting

Testing a trading strategy on historical data to evaluate behavior before live deployment.

## Event-Driven Backtest

A backtest style where market data, signals, orders, fills, and portfolio updates are processed as events.

## Vectorized Backtest

A backtest style that uses array operations over full time series, often making parameter sweeps faster.

## CTA

Commodity Trading Advisor. In this repository, CTA usually refers to futures-oriented trend following, timing, or systematic trading strategies.

## Factor

A feature or signal used to explain or predict asset returns, risk, or portfolio construction decisions.

## Walk-Forward Test

A validation method that repeatedly trains or selects parameters on past data and tests on later unseen data.
